## 📌 What is SwichPay? — What does this project do?

SwichPay is a hosted online payment gateway: open a merchant account yourself online, connect your website with one API, and get a signed notification the moment a customer pays by JazzCash, Easypaisa or Raast QR. It includes a merchant dashboard, invoices & payment links, payment recovery for interrupted checkouts, sub-merchant accounts for more websites, and an agent program that pays commission on referred merchants.

## 🎯 Who is it for?

E-commerce stores, SaaS & digital products, freelancers, education, ticketing and service businesses in Pakistan — plus agencies managing many sites and agents who refer merchants.

## ⭐ Key Features & Services — What it offers

- ✅ JazzCash, Easypaisa & Raast QR in one checkout and one API (banks & cards in same checkout)
- ✅ Hosted, mobile-ready checkout with your store name and logo
- ✅ Self-onboarding: register, submit KYC, get API keys, go live — no sales calls
- ✅ Instant signed IPN notifications with automatic retries + status API safety net
- ✅ Payment recovery — paid-but-interrupted checkouts are re-checked and confirmed
- ✅ Sub-merchant accounts: separate keys, transactions and withdrawals per website/client
- ✅ Agent program: referral link and commission credited to your agent wallet
- ✅ Merchant dashboard: transactions, reports, invoices & payment links, withdraw to bank
